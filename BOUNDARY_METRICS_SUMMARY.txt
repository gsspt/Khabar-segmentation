WHAT WENT WRONG
================================================================================

The model:
  1. Predicted 17,938 total "boundary" tokens (6.0% of all tokens)
  2. Top-K filtering kept 800 highest-confidence boundaries
  3. These 800 happened to divide text into ~600 segments
  4. Segment COUNT matched gold standard (582 ≈ 613) → reported as 94.9% recall
  5. BUT actual boundary POSITIONS don't match (only 15.3% correct)

Why this is misleading:
  - Segment count ≠ boundary correctness
  - Model got lucky: random boundaries happened to create right # of segments
  - Actual khabars are broken/misaligned at boundaries
